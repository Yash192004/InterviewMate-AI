# InterviewMate AI 🎯
### Your Personal AI Interview Coach

InterviewMate AI is an AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students and freshers prepare for job interviews and internships.

## ✨ Features
- **AI Resume Analyzer:** Analyzes resumes and identifies skills and projects.
- **Mock Interviews:** Generates personalized HR and technical questions.
- **Answer Evaluation:** Provides scores, feedback, and improved sample answers.
- **Adaptive Learning:** Identifies weak topics and adjusts future practice.
- **Progress Dashboard:** Tracks interview performance and improvement areas.
